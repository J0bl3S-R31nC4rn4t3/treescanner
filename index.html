How ScaniFlora Works

Swipe →
STEP 01 On-Field Tag

Identify the Tree

Locate a tree in any park, campus, or ecotourism site that has a unique ScaniFlora QR code tag attached to it.

STEP 02 Camera Reticle

Scan the QR Code

Use your smartphone camera inside ScaniFlora to scan the QR code on the tag.

STEP 03 Instant Profile

Access Information

Open the ScaniFlora app to view detailed botanical and conservation information about the tree.

STEP 04 Conservation

Learn & Appreciate

Explore the tree's significance, uses, and interesting facts provided in the app.

Logo Breakdown & Elements Meaning

ScaniFlora — Every tree has a story.

Scaniflora is an application that makes tree identification easier and more accessible, especially for non-forestry individuals, naturally curious people, and tourists visiting an ecotourism sites.
